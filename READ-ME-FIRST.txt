Pawn to Professor v1.9.8u — Subtle Game-Only Watermark

This replaces the ugly full-page repeated watermark idea.

WHAT IT DOES
- NO watermark on homepage.
- NO watermark on menus.
- NO watermark on Admin portal.
- NO repeated watermark across the whole screen.
- Only the secure game/player area is protected.

Inside the secure game player:
Top-right:
  pawntoprofessor.com

Bottom-left:
  Licensed to: <username>

The watermark is small and faint.

Protection is limited to the secure player:
- right-click blocked in the game area
- image/iframe dragging blocked
- Cmd/Ctrl+S blocked
- Cmd/Ctrl+P blocked

IMPORTANT
This still cannot guarantee screenshot prevention.
It is a deterrent and makes screenshots traceable to the member account.

INSTALL
1. Upload these 3 files to the website root:
   play.html
   game-watermark-v1.9.8u.js
   game-watermark-v1.9.8u.css

2. Replace the current play.html with this one.

3. Do NOT add anything to index.html.

4. Commit.
5. Wait for Vercel Ready.
6. Hard refresh.
7. Login with a normal member and open a secure activity.

NO SQL.
NO NEW VERCEL FUNCTION.
