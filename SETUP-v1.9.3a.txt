Pawn to Professor — v1.9.3a Legal Admin Fix
===============================================

PURPOSE
-------
Fix the missing:

⚖️ Legal & Registration

button in Admin.

This is a WEBSITE-ONLY patch.

DO NOT RUN SQL.
DO NOT CHANGE SUPABASE.
DO NOT REPLACE app.js.
DO NOT REPLACE legal.js.

WHAT THIS PATCH DOES
--------------------
It adds one final compatibility file:

legal-admin-fix.js

This file loads LAST and guarantees that the Admin menu contains:

⚖️ Legal & Registration

It also directly renders the editable legal editor, so it does not depend on
the older dynamic Admin-tab insertion working correctly.

The four existing legal documents remain:

- Common Terms of Use
- Teacher Terms of Use
- Learner Terms of Use
- Privacy Policy

Existing database legal versions and consent records are NOT changed.

INSTALLATION — STEP BY STEP
---------------------------

STEP 1
Open your GitHub repository:

pawn-to-professor-learning-hub

STEP 2
Upload / ADD this new file:

legal-admin-fix.js

Put it in the ROOT of the repository, beside:

app.js
legal.js
member-trial.js
index.html

STEP 3
REPLACE your current:

index.html

with the index.html included in this patch.

IMPORTANT:
Do not replace app.js.
Do not replace legal.js.
Do not run SQL.

STEP 4
Commit the two file changes.

Suggested commit message:

v1.9.3a fix Legal Registration admin menu

STEP 5
Open Vercel.

Wait until the new Production deployment says:

Ready

STEP 6
Hard-refresh the website.

Mac / Chrome:
Command + Shift + R

Windows:
Ctrl + F5

STEP 7
Login as Admin / Owner.

Press:

Admin

Scroll to the bottom of the Admin menu.

You should now see:

🧾 Audit Log
⚖️ Legal & Registration

STEP 8
Press:

⚖️ Legal & Registration

You should see:

Common Terms of Use
Teacher Terms of Use
Learner Terms of Use
Privacy Policy

Each document should have:

Preview
Save Draft
Publish New Version

- Unit access

- resources
